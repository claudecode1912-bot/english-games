прямая линия: Present Simple · волна: Present Continuous

Hi! My name is Lily, and this is my brother Tom. We are twins. Every Saturday we help our neighbor, Mrs Brown. She is old and she needs help with the house. She always gives us tips, a little money for our work.

Today it's raining. I can hear the rain clearly on the window. "No problem," Mrs Brown says. "I know something we can do inside."

Right now we are getting ready. Tom is putting on his old clothes, because our work is often dirty. I am choosing a suitable jumper. Not my new one!

Mrs Brown has an old wooden chair. One leg is broken. Tom loves to fix things, so at the moment he is repairing the chair. He is sticking the leg back with strong glue. "Don't break it again!" I laugh.

I am helping Mrs Brown in the kitchen. We bake cookies every Saturday, and today we are baking chocolate ones. The table is clean, but the bowls are dirty and the cookie jar is empty. We make thirty cookies. That's enough for each of us!

Then we start a new project. Mrs Brown gives us brushes and paper, and we paint at the same time. Tom is painting a dog, and I am painting the rain. Each of us paints our own picture, and we all enjoy it.

In the evening the rain stops, and the sky is clear. Biscuit, Mrs Brown's dog, is waiting at the door with his lead in his mouth. We take him for a walk. When we come back, Biscuit is wet, so we dry him with an old blanket.

Before we go home, Mrs Brown packs the cookies in a box. "This is for your mum," she says. We know it: Saturday is the best day of the week!

Present Simple или Present Continuous?

В истории есть оба времени: что Лили и Том делают обычно и что происходит прямо сейчас.

Present Simple

Обычно, всегда, каждую субботу: привычки, расписание, факты.

I / we / they bake · he / she / it bakes

Маркеры: always, usually, often, sometimes, never, every Saturday

We bake cookies every Saturday.

Present Continuous

Прямо сейчас, в этот момент, сегодня: действие идёт.

am / is / are + глагол-ing

Маркеры: now, right now, at the moment, today, Look!, Listen!

Today we are baking chocolate cookies.

Ловушка из нашего списка слов. Глаголы know, need, hear описывают состояние, а не действие, поэтому в Continuous их обычно не ставят, даже со словом now. Говорим I know, she needs help now, I can hear the rain. А enjoy можно: We are enjoying the walk!
